TAKEAWAY: For unweighted/unit-weight graphs, standard FIFO queue BFS runs in strictly
O(V + E) linear time. A binary heap introduces an unnecessary O(log V) priority queue
overhead per edge relaxation, making Dijkstra substantially slower and more memory-heavy.
